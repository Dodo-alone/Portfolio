PokerCalc

A hold'em calculator. Give it your cards, the table and any dead cards, and it returns win probability, hand distribution and EV against multiple opponents.

01 — Overview

The first iteration of this calculator was exact-only and hit a combinatorial wall despite clever symmetry tricks. With two opponents on the flop there are 483 million scenarios, which takes a long time to enumerate.

The rewrite splits the work in two. Exact enumeration handles the cases where it's tractable, and Monte Carlo sampling handles everything else. One sample result feeds equity, hand distribution and EV without re-dealing. 10k trials gives ±1%, and 40k gives ±0.5%.
