01 — Overview

!summarize gives you what happened since you were last active, and !summarize 50 covers the last 50 messages. The bot spots gaps in a conversation and treats them as breaks. It understands Discord-specific context like replies, reactions and media, so a summary keeps track of who said what to whom.

  • Rate limiting built around the free tier: 15 requests/min, 250k tokens/min and 1k requests/day, with token estimation before each call.
  • Long summaries are split cleanly by paragraph or newline to fit within Discord's message limits.
  • Admin-configurable system prompts and settings, per server.
  • Privacy: errors and rate limits are logged, but user messages and AI outputs never are. Users are offered an opt out, admins can configure on an opt in basis. No identifiable features are passed to the Gemini API.
