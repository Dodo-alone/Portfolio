01 — Simulated Economy

Most existing market simulators give agents an outside "fundamental value", usually a random walk or historical prices. Here the fundamental comes from inside the model. Firms hire labour, produce and sell goods, service debt and report earnings. Traders read those reports, form their own valuations and trade. Prices come out of the resulting order flow.

The goal is understanding, not prediction. Success means unprogrammed emergence: Okun's law and the Phillips curve reproduced from simple rules, realistic price action and order flow.

One early finding: single-path time averages don't converge, because the economy is non-ergodic. This means that any statistics produced must come from an ensemble run rather than one long one.

02 — Trading Algo Playground

A research environment for systematic equity strategies at 1-minute resolution. Known biases are either prevented by the engine or reported loudly in the results.

  • Look-ahead is impossible in the event-driven engine.
  • Each run reports the biases it couldn't prevent, with severities.
  • Parameter sweeps run in two tiers: a quick screen of the whole space, then full runs on the survivors.
